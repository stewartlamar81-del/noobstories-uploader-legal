Privacy Policy — NoobStories Uploader

Last updated: September 29, 2026

NoobStories Uploader is a personal utility built and operated by Lamar Stewart. Its sole purpose is to let the owner upload his own short videos to the drafts inbox of his own TikTok account, so he can review and post them from the TikTok app.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok Login Kit (basic profile): when the owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok shares basic profile information (such as open ID and display name) so the app can confirm which account is connected.
  • Video files the owner selects: the app uploads video files chosen by the owner to his own TikTok drafts inbox via TikTok's Content Posting API. Videos are never sent anywhere else.

How data is stored

Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally on the owner's own computer in a private file. They are never transmitted to any third party, analytics service, or advertising network.

What the app does NOT do

  • No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • The app is for the owner's personal use only; no other users' data is collected.

Content responsibility

The owner is solely responsible for the videos he uploads and for complying with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
